# 1. Brainstorming & Ideation

## Project Title
EduGenie: Google Gemini Powered Learning Assistant

## Problem Statement
Students often find it difficult to understand complex topics, prepare notes, revise lessons and create practice questions before examinations.

## Proposed Idea
EduGenie is an AI-powered learning assistant that uses Google Gemini to help students understand educational topics in a simple and interactive way.

## Main Ideas

### 1. Ask EduGenie
Students can ask questions and receive simple explanations.

### 2. Simple Explanation
Complex topics can be explained in easy, student-friendly language.

### 3. Summarize Notes
Students can provide long study material and get short and useful summaries.

### 4. Quiz Generator
EduGenie can generate practice questions and multiple-choice questions from a given topic.

### 5. Exam Preparation
The system can help students revise important concepts and prepare for examinations.

## Target Users
- School students
- College students
- Students preparing for examinations

## Expected Outcome
EduGenie will provide students with a simple AI-based learning companion that supports learning, revision and exam preparation.
